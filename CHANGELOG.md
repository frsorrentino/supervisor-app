# Changelog

Pre-releases of the phone app, newest first; each one has its APK in the [releases](https://github.com/frsorrentino/supervisor-app/releases). No numbered versions yet: a release is named after what it brings and its date.

## 30/10/2026

- `home-v3-6`: reorder the home from the settings; the master's suggestions become a collapsible section.
- `home-v3-5`: the suggestions card in the new style, after the sessions.
- `home-v3-4`: the whole account name on the Usage row; new home order.
- `home-v3-3`: the System row no longer squeezes its title.
- `home-v3-2`: «l'8%» and «l'11%» in the week origins title.
- `home-v3`: the home in the new night style: waiting, sessions, work, outcomes, more; a board with the 5-hour quota.

## 29/10/2026

- `box-home`: the three boxes on top of the home open their page.
- `kpi-dati`, `kpi`, `kpi-piccole`: figures from the relay: hours, day bands, day totals, quota per session.
- `plugin-skill`: a «Plugins and skills» section on the home, per account.
- `observe`: plugin notes among the suggestions.

## 28/10/2026

- `sistema`, `sistema-stuck`: a «System» section with load, memory and advice; stuck processes outside the load.
- `chat-reset`: after a restart or /clear the chat takes the new conversation.
- `dispositivi`: paired devices with last seen and Revoke.
- `diff-piu`: one + icon for diffs everywhere.

## 27/10/2026

- `suggerimenti-coda`: suggestions in a queue, three at a time, new kinds.
- `chiusura-testo`: the state line no longer squeezes while a session closes.

## 26/10/2026

- `utilizzo-settimana`, `utilizzo-ritmo`: the week hour by hour; the pace of the 5-hour window.
- `sugg-focus`: a suggestion no longer enters the field on tap.

## 25/10/2026

- `limite-modello`: continue with another model when one hits its limit.
- `quota-settimana`: when the weekly quota reaches 100% at the current pace.
- `parliamone`: a «Let's talk» button on suggestions.

## 24/10/2026

- `home-suggerimenti`, `scelta-notte-home`, `scheda-master`, `mcp-lavori`: suggestions on the home, the master's sheet, MCP questions.
- `notte-chiuse`, `notte-sessioni`: night cards open the session that worked, read-only when closed.
- `chiudi-feedback`: closing from a card gives feedback.

## 23/10/2026 and before

- `lavori-schede`, `scelte-e-schede`, `lavori-sessioni`, `carica-precedenti`, `fatto-home`, `fatto-1-80`, `done-60s`, `coda-ferme`, `avviso-chiudibile`: work items as cards, choices as buttons, «Done» on stopped sessions.
- `lavori`, `lavori-gerarchia`, `tab-emoji`: the single Work page (22/10).
- `impegni`, `impegni-b`, `notte`: updates inside the app, the night view (21/10 and 22/10).
